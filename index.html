A Ranked Reading List

TOP 10 ANIME
OF ALL TIME

Ten series that shaped what anime can be — picked for craft, influence, and the way they've stuck with audiences long after the credits rolled.

10
Gintama 2006–2018 · Sunrise · 367 eps

A samurai comedy that spends half its time on toilet humor and the other half delivering some of the most devastating dramatic arcs in the medium. Its willingness to swing between absurd and heartbreaking without warning is what earns it a spot here.

9
Code Geass 2006–2008 · Sunrise · 50 eps

A mecha political thriller built around a prince who can command absolute obedience with a single look. It's soapy, calculated, and unafraid of consequences — few shows commit to their endgame as fully as this one does.

8
Demon Slayer 2019– · ufotable · 55 eps

A fairly familiar shonen skeleton — orphan, sword, tournament arcs — elevated by animation that treats every fight like a hand-painted set piece. It's the show that proved modern anime could look like nothing else on television.

7
Cowboy Bebop 1998 · Sunrise · 26 eps

A jazz-scored bounty hunter drama that never overstays its welcome. Every episode is a self-contained mood piece, and the show's willingness to let its characters simply be lonely, rather than explain it, still feels rare.

6
One Piece 1999– · Toei · 1000+ eps

Absurdly long, and that's the point — few stories have had the runway to build a world this dense or pay off setups planted decades earlier. The scale is the achievement.

5
Steins;Gate 2011 · White Fox · 24 eps

A time-travel thriller that spends its first half as a slow hangout comedy, then tightens into one of the most quietly devastating back halves in anime. The payoff earns every minute of setup.

4
Hunter x Hunter (2011) 2011–2014 · Madhouse · 148 eps

A show that keeps redefining what kind of story it's telling — adventure, tournament, then a descent into one of the darkest arcs ever put to screen. Its power system is also one of the few that rewards close reading.

3
Death Note 2006–2007 · Madhouse · 37 eps

A cat-and-mouse thriller between a killer and the detective hunting him, told almost entirely through dialogue and glances. It made courtroom-logic tension feel as high-stakes as any battle series.

2
Attack on Titan 2013–2023 · WIT / MAPPA · 94 eps

Starts as a monster show about walls and giants, ends as one of the most morally complicated war stories anime has told. Few series have escalated their scope this dramatically without losing the plot.

1
Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood 2009–2010 · Bones · 64 eps

Two brothers, a broken law of equivalent exchange, and a story that treats every character — hero or villain — as someone worth understanding. It balances comedy, war, and philosophy better than almost anything else in the medium, and rarely wastes an episode.

This ranking reflects one perspective, weighing craft, ambition, and lasting influence — not a universal scoreboard. Ask ten anime fans for their top 10 and you'll get ten different lists, and that's part of the fun.
